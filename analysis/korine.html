The Harmony Korine interview archive

Harmony Korine doesn’t really give many interviews these days, and the old ones aren’t exactly easy to track down. Luckily, someone was collecting them before they disappeared. In the early 2000s, a fan site called harmony-korine.com (unrelated to the better-known harmonykorine.com, which also had archives of its own) built a collection of print and online interviews stretching from the press around Gummo in 1995 to the aftermath of Julien Donkey-Boy. Along the way, there’s Larry Clark taking a swipe at his own protégé, Bruce LaBruce reporting from a film set, and even a Girl Scout newsletter landing an exclusive in 2004. Crazy stuff! The site itself is long gone, but its interview index survives in the Wayback Machine.

This archive collects links, excerpts, and transcripts of interviews and articles, primarily sourced from the February 12, 2007 Wayback Machine snapshot of harmony-korine.com, with the archived home.comcast.net/~daken page used for the Internet Chat transcript. Material here is presented for archival, educational, and critical research purposes, with attribution and links to available original sources. Copyright remains with the respective authors, interviewers, publishers, and other rights holders. This archive is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Where possible, consult the original publications for the complete works.

A note on the texts: although the interviews appear in this site’s design, their original markup has been preserved as extracted, and so some typos may be included.
